# BrineSpace reusable character bodies

Krusher Dom is the reference trial: System Administrator, AI & Robotics, matte muted cyan #527C80. Choose a body by build, silhouette and equipment. The owner's proposed default for female characters is Veld. Preserve individual likeness rather than treating any body as a universal head-swap template.

| Family | Character variation | Evidence from this trial | Still needs individual work |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bill | Broad suit | Four-direction bare/helmet idle and walk; west terminal action; movement-driven gait comparison | New likeness, directional heads, facial polish and any additional role actions |

1. Freeze the selected source manifests and images. Keep originals and hashes.
2. Keep source canvas, foot pivot and standing ruler. These trial bodies use184x184, pivot(92,172), standing148px. Keep each family's own frame counts, gait timing and strides.
3. Prepare independent front, rear and side heads at source density. Match the neck anchor; do not enlarge a head to disguise a body mismatch. Side anchors follow the neck rather than the nose.
4. Preserve complete suit collar pixels as a foreground layer. Track source head/collar motion before extending a fit across frames. A horizontal cut is only an early static experiment.
5. Recolour department markings with a suit-specific mask. Bill red, James yellow, Veld blue and Marsh olive shoulders use different rules. Preserve head RGB, alpha, tools and utility colours. Matte cyan shading should remain subdued.
6. Fit the face to each visor interior while retaining the existing shell and glass silhouette. Profiles show the forward face; the rear shell hides identity. Source face edges can still need polish.
7. Compare representative idle, walk and the intended role action. Inspect transitions and full motion at the normal display size. Byte checks and matching clocks support technical compatibility; they do not establish natural motion by themselves.
8. Package candidates separately. Check the existing sprite player before changing normal selection. Actual NPC behavior and station integration require their own requested scope and checks.

All four examples are alternatives. They do not share a gait clock, action catalog or every collar/helmet mask. Current game selections remain unchanged. Blink studies remain separate from both runtime packages.
