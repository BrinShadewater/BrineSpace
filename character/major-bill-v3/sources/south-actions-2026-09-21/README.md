# Bill south action sources

Selected source recipes for south kneel, repair and reversed stand. Built-in image
generation produced the two raw sheets; exact prompts are alongside them. No
Higgsfield. Original source contracts and older art remain preserved.

`tools/build_bill_south_actions.py` reads these registrations through the canonical
`tools/rebuild_bill_art.py` provenance callbacks. It extracts six poses at a common
scale, corrects pose 1's planted lower leg, retains the settled body outside the
repair-tool region and fits the existing front helmet. Repair begins/ends at the
exact settled kneel frame. Stand reverses kneel. Timing and pivots stay unchanged.

The JSON status strings retain candidate-stage history; this folder is the selected
canonical recipe. Integration evidence and current acceptance are in
`docs/BILL_ACTION_SEQUENCE_2026-09-21.md` and `docs/CURRENT_STATUS.md`. Owner motion
acceptance remains open. Other directions are outside this source repair.
