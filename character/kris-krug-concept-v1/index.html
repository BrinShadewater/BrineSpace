Kris Krüg

Cognitive Concordance Specialist
AI & Robotics · Speciality: Intent Harmonisation

Kris reconciles conflicting machine directives with crew needs. He helps BRINE and the station’s autonomous systems agree on what they are trying to accomplish—and whether those objectives still serve the people living there.

His signature equipment is a link tablet and harness recorder. The asymmetric charcoal wrap suit, pale collar and muted cyan shoulder place him in AI & Robotics while keeping his silhouette distinct.

“He was assigned to align my objectives. We are still discussing whose.” — proposed BRINE introduction
