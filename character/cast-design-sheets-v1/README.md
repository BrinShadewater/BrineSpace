# Cast design sheets

Owner requested design sheets for the other characters after selecting Veld's Balanced direction with slightly lighter skin. Scope: Bill, Branforth, Marsh, Margot, Josh and River. BRINE's core and accepted portrait assets are outside this character-animation reference set.

Human proportions follow the balanced adult direction while preserving their established identities. Companion silhouettes remain character-specific. Generated sheets are design-reference candidates for owner review, not evidence of installed animations or exact indexed palettes. The existing locked runtime palettes remain unchanged.

Identity requirements:
- Bill: sturdy officer, short brown hair, full beard, graphite/burgundy suit.
- Branforth: older stocky engineer, greying hair, horseshoe moustache, forehead goggles, graphite/burnt-orange suit and wrench.
- Marsh: clean-shaven blond male android, subtle temple implant, ivory/sage armour, no helmet.
- Margot: adult white/tabby cat, blue-grey eyes, her right muzzle patch, moss-green frog bonnet.
- Josh: lavender rectangular head, amber eyes, articulated claws, charcoal tread chassis.
- River: rounded ivory/sage inspection robot, large amber lens, lateral wheel pods, no added arms or human legs.

Source references, full prompts and generated files are recorded alongside the sheets. Printed material swatches describe colour families; after visual selection, sample the actual selected artwork into an explicit production palette rather than trusting generated labels as numeric colour specifications.

Production gate: verify front/side/rear anatomy, equipment side, repeated feature placement and material shades before deriving animation. Keep source sheets frozen by hash when selected. Do not regenerate each action independently or use a motion source as identity authority. No runtime sprite, palette, portrait or gameplay changes in this design-sheet step.
