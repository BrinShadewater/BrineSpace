October 3, 2026. Full goal remains active and incomplete.

The current loader resolves 198 bare and 192 helmet states from 118 manifests,
with 4,804 unique selected sources and no missing referenced files. These are
coverage counts, not visual or gameplay acceptance. Equipment-only actions and

## Reviewed evidence

Thirteen boards viewed: four cardinal dry-master boards (112 presentations),
four dry/water boards (80), two furniture boards (48), and three complete bare
West/North/South interact/repair sequences (36). Total 276 presentations, with
overlap. Images retain original colour and native scale; only pivot padding is
used. East/West authored standing foundations and original North/South masters
are references. The North original retains some magenta edge contamination;
its prepared clean version is an authored cleanup, not pixel-identical authority.
Bare master columns assess body identity, not helmet construction.

## Confirmed repair priorities

1. **West/North/South interact and repair.** Every six-pose bare row reviewed.
   Interact starts/ends with the detailed selected standing figure but poses
   1–4 switch to softer, cooler art with different head/hair, suit panels and
   equipment. All six repair poses share that softer family. This is a source
   identity/detail discontinuity, not merely a timing or colour-filter problem.
   Selected authority: `veld-action-{west,north,south}-matched-v1/manifest.json`.
   Repair whole source poses against original directional masters; preserve
   action meaning and appropriate kneeling height rather than equalizing bounds.
2. **Cargo bodies across cardinals.** Sampled West/South cargo heads, markings,
   highlights and stance differ from idle/master. North cargo changes rear
   equipment and lower-body construction. East is closer but still needs complete
   motion, support and standing joins. Existing staged original-source studies
   remain evidence; do not automatically replace selected art with those studies.
3. **Furniture.** East/West sit/read art differs visibly in finish, face/hair and
   helmet construction from standing. North read-seated is softer/cooler than
   North sit-idle; South sampled sit/read is more coherent with its selected idle.
   Side rows may be dormant: verify actual dispatch before prioritizing admission.
   Sprite boards cannot establish chair depth, seat height or physical contact.
4. **Walk/idle and water cross-family polish.** Selected South idle skin is darker
   than original; South walk has a different front stance/head. North master edge
   tint complicates global colour comparison. Sampled water families share more
   consistent internal construction but still require master/perspective/detail
   review, tread/distress/death sequences and actual medium transitions.

test was warranted for this static audit. Water and furniture sampling does not
accept entire families. Helmet rotation fidelity remains open; these boards do
not establish a mid-turn helmet defect because turns were not examined here.
