# Veld consistency findings — 2026-09-29

The staged fitting/removal candidate has drifted from the selected Veld idles and
walks. Similar cloth medians and exact idle endpoints did not establish visual
consistency. Another palette-only adjustment will leave the source/style mismatch.

## Reference and coverage

Resolved the current catalog followed by the Veld overrides in grid_canvas.gd,
rather than treating every folder as accepted artwork. The selected library has
173 bare states (20 revised, 153 retained) and 167 equipped states (24 revised,
143 retained). Decoded and hashed 2,932 selected frame references. Fifteen contact
sheets show first/middle samples from every selected state. All sheets and the
large east comparison were visually inspected. Eight complete directional core
sequences are available for playback review; full motion acceptance of all 340
states is not claimed. Runtime-derived joins are outside this inventory.

## Direction for the next fitting revision

Use selected bare and helmet directional idle/walk as the identity, density,
material and helmet authority. Preserve these assets. Freeze v13 as rejected for
consistency, retaining its useful timing and action staging. Rebuild fitting from
the accepted east source/body/head, preserving canonical stationary regions and
authoring only the required arms/helmet motion. Raised-arm occlusion and shell
placement require visual work; do not pretend recoloring or pasted endpoints
solves them. Compare the first interior pose, all fitting/removal phases and joins
against the reference together at 82/54/27px before expanding or installing.
