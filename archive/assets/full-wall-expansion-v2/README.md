# Additional full-wall installations

Selected source art and exact prompts for Tidal Condenser, Biodome and Thermal Power Control. `manifest.json` records selected/rejected images, SHA-256 hashes, dimensions, registrations and native card provenance.

Each room has a horizontal view and separate inward-facing east/west art. Tidal and Thermal also have north-facing south-wall banks. Biodome uses its horizontal bank on a sealed north wall in either east/west corridor orientation.

White backgrounds and reviewed enclosed apertures are excluded by vector registration. Raster source pixels are unchanged. Rejected candidates remain for provenance and are not selected at runtime.

See `docs/FULL_WALL_EXPANSION_2026-09-08.md` for integration and validation.
