# Mining rover animation contract

Accepted v10 four-wheel mining rover, driving on the seabed; no swimming,
hovering or propellers. Preserve suspension, central processing bay, muted cyan,
matte steel, four large studded tyres and overhead/shallow-front camera.

Native room/crew scale and the south-facing pilot were reviewed before expansion.
Completed review coverage: eight headings for idle/start/drive/reverse/steer
left/steer right/stop/loaded drive/power on/power off/charging/disabled;
four cardinals for mining/intake/unload/blocked recovery. Total: 112 clips.
The complete work-cycle preview chains mine, intake, loaded travel and unload.
No automatic mirroring. Ground pivot stays fixed; wheel tread travels along the
tyre surface, never spins the entire wheel silhouette. Mining braces the rover
and deploys a tool; tool effects stay separate. Top lamp remains separate with
blue operational, red stopped and yellow charging states.

Export: 448x448 RGBA, pivot (224,224), 30fps; 0.34 world units/library pixel.
Proposed width 246px / 83.64 world units, derived from the historical 103:85
Mining:Construction ratio and current construction ~69 units. Provisional until
room/crew review, not a navigation or physical-size acceptance. Original v10
103px width was a launch-study component, not the NPC footprint.

Review pack only: no game roles, resources, save data or launch/recovery changes.
