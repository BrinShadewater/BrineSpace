# Giant undersea mushrooms

Original ivory-blue umbrella, amethyst tiered colony and coral-gold fungus. Broad scalloped cap tops, overlapping satellites and small fungal buttons communicate giant size in the strict overhead camera. Matte painted detail follows the installed low fungal shelves.

Canopy widths are 1.25, 1.45 and 1.30 room cells. Sparse placements follow saved habitat regions and avoid the station core, including the canopy's half-width clearance. These static decorative organisms have no collision or resource state.

Versioned masters, exact prompts and immutable hashes are preserved. `bake.py` trims transparent margins and resizes isotropically to 1024px width without colour grading or alpha repaint. Existing underwater tint is applied by the renderer.

`tools/review_giant_mushrooms.ps1` captures an isolated native density comparison against low fungi, each giant beside rooms at three zooms, and lit habitat views through the production seabed renderer. It also runs the environment suite and fingerprints the real owner profile before and after.
