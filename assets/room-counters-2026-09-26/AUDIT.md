# Perspective, density and style check

September 26, 2026. Scope: the four latest room counters. Compared native exports with the accepted science cabinet and earlier drawer counter; see audit-comparison.png. No artwork modified.

## Findings

- Perspective: broadly consistent overhead/cutaway facing, visible worktops, parallel front/rear edges and no conflicting sideways yaw. The medical counter has a taller front relative to its top than the earlier drawer counter. This reads as a proportion difference, not enough evidence to assert a different camera angle. Match heights explicitly if these are later intended to join into continuous runs.
- Pixel density: all four use exactly the same declared ratio, approximately 2.94 source pixels per world unit, at their manifest display widths. Visible widths are galley 260, medical 245, specimens 260 and engineering 280 library pixels. All export hashes match. This verifies export convention, not live physical size or crew fit.
- Art style: coherent muted hand-painted steel/composite materials, readable seams and controlled surface texture. Accessories are appropriately simplified at export size. No broad style correction needed.
- Optional finish polish: the engineering pump bands and galley knife/pot edges carry stronger light accents than the cabinet surfaces. They read as metal, but could be softened slightly if a more uniformly matte finish is desired. No obvious glossy treatment across the set.

The counters are visually compatible individual props. They are not verified interchangeable modules: cabinet heights, worktop depths and support designs vary. Wall contact and character scale remain untested in-game. Preserve existing exports pending any requested correction.
