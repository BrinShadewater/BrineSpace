## Registration and production

Selected sources are cropped by alpha and uniformly scaled using central viewport
diameter as a common feature. No whole-drone direction is a rotation or mirror.
`build_full.py` records per-heading anchors, fan ellipses and jaw pivots. The
articulation masks preserve both support arms; tapered wrist masks avoid cutout
slivers in closed diagonal poses. This produces painted directional approximations,
not geometrically exact projections of a shared 3D model. Review perspective and
relative side-view proportions visually before accepting the set.
