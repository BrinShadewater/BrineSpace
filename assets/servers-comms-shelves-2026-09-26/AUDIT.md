# Pixel density, style and perspective audit

September 26, 2026. Scope: all six props in this pack. Compared native-size exports with accepted science cabinetry and wood bookcase on light/dark backgrounds; see audit-light.png and audit-dark.png. No artwork modified.

## Pixel density
All six exports use approximately 2.94 source pixels per world unit at their proposed manifest display widths. Master and export hashes match for all six; transparent outer borders pass. Per-asset results are in audit-checks.json. Fine vent lines and shelf contents remain readable at native export size. This establishes consistent export convention, not final physical scale, runtime zoom or crew usability.

## Perspective
Shared straight-on overhead/cutaway family: visible top faces, horizontal fronts and no conflicting sideways yaw. Tall server fronts occupy more height than the low pedestal, consistent with different object proportions. The wall communications panel is more frontal and has only a shallow top bevel; this is plausible for a shallow wall-mounted housing but requires comparison against an actual room wall before accepting its mounting perspective. Floor-facing props show no clear perspective defect.

## Art style
Overall coherent muted painted steel, controlled local colors and subdued screen graphics. The science-style supply shelf is especially close to the reference. Personal shelving is simpler and lighter in wood treatment than the accepted ornate bookcase, which is appropriate for generic utility furniture; no need to copy its brass trim or richer decoration.

Minor polish candidate: diagonal scuff streaks across the tall server are more noticeable than wear on most other new props. They could be softened for a cleaner finish. Some shelf-container bands have brighter metallic highlights, comparable to the accepted science containers; no broad gloss problem found. No wholesale redraw indicated.

## Remaining checks
Actual wall-panel mounting and character-relative dimensions remain unverified. These are individual props, not a matched modular rack system. Preserve originals pending requested revisions.
