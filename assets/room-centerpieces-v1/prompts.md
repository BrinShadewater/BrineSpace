# Room centerpiece generation prompts

These are the production prompts used for the September 12, 2026 room furnishing pass. The two rejected candidates are retained because their silhouettes led directly to the corrected square and rectangular replacements.

## Maintenance repair gantry

Create one isolated 2D game prop sprite for BrineSpace: a large low hydraulic repair gantry for a maintenance bay. Fixed near-top-down cutaway camera, chunky detailed pixel art, screen-facing south front, height screen-up. Make the footprint a strong wide rectangle with an open center, four short hydraulic corner posts, a clamped mechanical assembly, compact service arms, tool trays, hoses contained on the chassis, burnt-orange panels, charcoal steel, and restrained pale metal. It must read as crew-scale workshop equipment rather than a room or vehicle. Matte surfaces, restrained highlights, localized handling wear, neutral ambient light, no baked glow, no text, no people, no floor, no wall, no shadow plane. Isolate the complete silhouette on actual transparent alpha with generous clear margin. Square canvas.
