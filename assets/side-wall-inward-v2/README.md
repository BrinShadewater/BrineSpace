# Inward-facing, darker side-wall revision

Owner correction: every operator-facing element must point into the room, and the latest colors should be slightly darker. These six selected sheets replace the twelve first-pass side registrations. Exact prompts, source hashes, dimensions and prior/rejected candidates are in manifest.json. Sources are RGB, with white exterior removed from rendering by polygon registration; PNG pixels are preserved.

The revision moves usable fronts to the inner long edge: research screens and cabinet access, medical autoclave and supply doors, Xeno screens/gloves/sideways microscope, isolation levers and wheel, pressure dial, and listening displays/keyboards. Short ends use closed caps. Visible top surfaces and skylights remain legible from the fixed overhead cutaway camera. Materials use muted grey-ivory, deep teal/navy/plum, dull brass and rust/ochre; brightness was reviewed in rooms rather than inferred from the white source background.

Research/Medical initial corrections retained south-facing interfaces and were rejected. Xeno required an eyepiece correction and then restoring a glove inadvertently changed into a tube. All intermediates are separately labeled, not runtime selections.

Native evidence: output/side-wall-inward-v2/inward-q1.png and inward-q3.png. Full-wall review and twelve-variant regression pass. Crew-route result is logged in output/side-wall-inward-routes.log. Horizontal assets/cards retain their previous selection. No exported build.
