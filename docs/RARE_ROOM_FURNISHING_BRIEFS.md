# Rare room furnishing briefs

The current inherited layouts are prototypes. Native route evidence is in `output/rare-room-catalog-routes-v3`; this does not approve their composition. All three have a canonical south entrance, rotated with the room.

## Pressure Control Chamber

Engineering steel with subdued amber indicators. Build a pressure-testing work area around the tank manifold: a supported gauge board, spare seals on a small maintenance bench, and short hoses ending at actual valves. Keep a clear approach from the entrance to controls. Replace inherited Life Support floor routing with dedicated runs; do not stretch hoses diagonally across the central aisle.

## Deepwater Listening Post

Science instrumentation with restrained cool displays. Group receiver racks with an operator desk and a north-facing seat. Put headphones on hooks, recordings and notebooks on a shelf, and sample signal charts on the desk. Route cabling along the sealed wall to the listening equipment. Preserve the quiet atmosphere and the open entrance approach; do not add unrelated laboratory clutter.

## Emergency Isolation Vault

Engineering reserve equipment with muted emergency markings. Group reserve banks and isolators along one working perimeter, with a test bench nearby and a sealed emergency-supply cabinet. Use short protected cable runs and a visible service cover where a run crosses circulation. Its inherited Battery Array routes currently produce unsuitable long diagonal connections and must be replaced with dedicated routing.

For each room, the remaining deliverables are a dedicated composition/profile, accurate texture dependencies, a selected native card, every furniture front checked in four rotations, and a normal-scale station review. New props need preserved source/provenance and independent alpha checks. Furniture and floor decoration do not imply new gameplay interactions.
