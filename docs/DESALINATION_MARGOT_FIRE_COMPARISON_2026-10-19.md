Ambient controls receive no inserted fire. Normal sprinklers buy 5 s of spray for 1 stored Water in each burning room. Paid spray continues with an empty reserve; zero stored Water does not itself mean a dry sprinkler or a lost run. A common policy requests ordinary full hull repair through the inspector at least 2 s after each fire ends, retries when Metal is short, and lets normal crew tasks/travel determine completion. No fire/hull/crew progress is supplied.

Where rescue-first fails, the retained trace must be read as a coupled emergency: insufficient suppression can keep Mining/Hydroponics offline, reducing new Metal and Oxygen while paid construction/crew access also competes with recovery. Stuck-job logs are evidence of lost progress, not proof of a new pathfinding bug or that every possible human response is unwinnable. This policy keeps sprinklers enabled and repairs extinguished compartments; it does not claim to exhaust alternate routes, cancellation, earlier prevention or other drafts. Stored Water, prepaid spray and actually functioning water production are distinct measures.

In the corrected failed seed 28417, the live guidance reports “Path blocked / crew cannot reach the ward door.” Recovery welding reaches 14.2 of 18 seconds and stops; final Metal is 6. The strict rescue-first policy never starts Desalination because Margot is not aboard. This makes adaptive emergency reprioritization or overlapping the water build with the rescue useful separate follow-ups; the failure is not evidence that the 8-Metal price alone needs changing. Rescue-first double-fire recovery times in the aggregate table describe the two successful recoveries; the third does not complete. Both other rescue-first double-fire runs survive, with 89.6 summed dry sprinkler room-seconds and 64.8 s to extinction, versus 12.9 s and no dry spray in all water-first cases.

This cohort supports preparing water infrastructure before the second recovery when emergency reserves are thin. It also records the cost in companion pacing. Keep the 8-Metal recovery price and existing Desalination/sprinkler values on this evidence; no numeric tuning or new recovery gate is implemented. Natural hazard frequency and first-time-player understanding remain separate design/playtest questions.
