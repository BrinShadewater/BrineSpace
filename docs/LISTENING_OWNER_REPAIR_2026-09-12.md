# Listening Post owner asset repair

Updated September 12, 2026. The live Deepwater Listening Post now uses one
directional visual family in all four orientations. The accepted south Deepwater
bank supplies a north-facing counterpart through an exact 180-degree rotation;
east and west retain their authored overhead banks. All keyboards, controls and
service edges face the room interior.

The old north-only U-shaped installation and bright blue/white north console remain
in source history but are no longer selected by the live room. The room again uses
its independent acoustic receiver, transducer and service equipment in q0, matching
the other orientations. A listening-only derivative of the shared radio equipment
atlas changes saturated red trim to muted ochre/brass. Geometry, footprints,
activity targets, screen effects and the Radio Lab consumer are unchanged.

Native Godot captures for q0-q3 are in
`output/listening-owner-repair-2026-09-12/final-native`. The refreshed live catalog
card is the reviewed q0 capture. Source hashes and deterministic operations are in
`assets/listening-directional-v2/directional-build.json`,
`equipment-palette-repair.json` and `review.json`.

Validation covers preferred room layouts, side-wall variants, all 47 catalog card
identities and native rare operating effects. No gameplay logic, economy, character
asset or animation behavior changed.
