Updated: September 12, 2026. Active objective: all rooms, including missing wall

## Scope and acceptance

The [coverage ledger](../assets/room-facing-rollout/coverage.json) inventories all
47 native RoomDatabase identities and four directions each. It distinguishes
existing full-wall registrations from unresolved other artwork/missing views.
Missing full-wall registration is not proof that a room has no artwork: split
banks and custom hosts must be traced before authoring. Corridors retain the
owner-cleared architecture and receive a fit review, not unsolicited machinery.

Each furnished room needs inward appliance access, side-wall contact, flush north
fit with overheight riser overlap, and overhead south art. Existing functioning
central equipment remains. Completion requires room-native evidence for each
applicable direction, source provenance, layout/crew checks and current consumers.
Registration or generation alone is not completion.
