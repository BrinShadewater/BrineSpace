# Character continuation and handoff

Begin from the current selected manifests and source hashes. Parent READMEs and
old captures may lag actor-specific revisions. Reconcile the concise current
summary rather than presenting every historical TODO as still missing.

Track independently: generated poses, registered exports, runtime selection,
sampled native playback, actual route clearance, continuous visual review and
packaged integration. A pixel-equality check does not approve a silhouette or a
transition. Single-actor doorway fixtures do not prove concurrent traffic or
actual exterior traversal.

Equipment review must include the whole sequence: bare idle, approach/pickup,
donning, equipped idle/locomotion, removal, deposit and return to bare idle.
Correct pivots cannot fix changing body proportions or helmet design. Once pickup
and deposit are integrated, describe remaining joins rather than calling those
actions absent. Preserve actor-specific sources when rebuilding derived returns.

For crew movement/save fixes, record every actor's seed and controller/source/PCK
identity. Separate matching primary-actor traces from full peer determinism.
Retain timeouts and interrupted runs even if a later seed passes. Do not freeze
peers or widen arrival tolerances to manufacture traversal acceptance.

At a credit-limited pause, save the coverage, exact unresolved joins, strongest
current evidence and next bounded action; avoid another generation batch. Update
maintained skill references and installed mirrors. Structural skill validation
and a partial fresh-context trial are not a completed behavior evaluation.
