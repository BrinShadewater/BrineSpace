Consolidated September 8, 2026. Detailed trials remain in asset READMEs and docs/FITTED_SERVICE_SESSION_CLOSEOUT_2026-09-08.md.

## Direction and materials

Check cabinet backing/pulls and every fixture independently. Correct tester knobs or case latches can coexist with a backwards cabinet. For asymmetric machinery verify the usable opening: sewing wheel/needle endpoints alone did not prove operator facing.

Preserve the fixed top-down camera when redrawing directions. Record changes in depth, socket counts and drawers. Four views do not establish identical collision footprints or exact rotation. Reduce equipment as well as cabinet depth; preserve working space. Bright rim lines, bolts and cylindrical rail stripes require material correction, not global darkening.

## Fit before expansion

Review proposed hosts with authored furniture retained and actual open sides; isolate personal layout overrides. Visual rectangles and schematic door bays are diagnostics. Zero overlaps do not establish mount planes, physics or occupied access.

When a full bank conflicts, author a shorter functional section with finished ends for a measured gap. Do not crop through machinery, hide existing furniture or shrink a whole bank until its equipment is unusable. The100-unit communications section clears the tested Radio Lab visual overlaps; installation remains pending.
