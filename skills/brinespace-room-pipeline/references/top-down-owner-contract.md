# Current owner contract: top-down and inward

Accepted after owner gameplay and Layout Studio review, September 12, 2026.
This is the current camera contract. It supersedes older fixed-south, front-elevation,
tall north-wall and side-only completion guidance wherever they conflict.

Read docs/TOP_DOWN_ART_DIRECTION_2026-09-12.md and docs/ROOM_ART_HANDOFF_2026-09-12.md
in the active BrineSpace checkout. The verbatim notes and source-selection snapshot
are under docs/handoffs/2026-09-12-top-down/. Do not interpret old passing tests as
acceptance under this new direction.

All equipment is top-down and operates toward room center on every wall. North may
have restrained perspective only if low and consistent through rotation. Preserve
visible inward controls, tool grips and access; keep hinges/hoses toward the wall.
Observation portholes belong to the background wall/riser, not movable equipment.
Use the owner's preferred south examples to establish coherent four-wall sets.

Preserve department identity and native crew scale; reduce bright orange to local
machinery colors, eliminate white cutouts and metallic glare. Cold Store moves blue;
medical beds use matching blue; Biomass green and matching Tidal/Command walls matter.
The queue contains explicit removals, new furnishings, gameplay reports and unresolved
wording; do not silently reinterpret or drop these during an art-only pass.

Workflow: identify live/Studio consumers -> inventory/camera/size brief -> generate
with exact prompt and preserved originals -> source component review -> vector or
true-alpha registration -> wall/crew fit -> all-orientation native review -> relevant
power/retained/pause checks -> reviewed cards and current bindings -> evidence/ledger.
Preserved collision bounds alone do not prove acceptable scale. RGBA channels or
filenames do not prove transparency; inspect actual alpha/background and apertures.

Verified lessons consolidated in the direction document: do not inherit elevation
camera from the sole reference; count across the whole bank; inspect every tool grip;
keep separately authored mates when warranted; outer-edge-align contain frames;
route metadata/mirroring through every consumer; use source-local runtime emissions;
keep reading lamps steady; isolate each effect; distinguish actual station pause from
fixed-clock captures; distinguish four repeated fixed views from four orientations.

At handoff, preserve raw notes, selected-source evidence and open work. No generation
is required for closeout. Sync only your maintained changes into the installed skill.
