Keep catalogs, original sources and personal layouts. The later riser decision also pauses separate wall decorations in Studio, including airlock fittings; do not restore them as part of another cleanup. ## Source and scale lessons

- Judge handheld accessories beside an actual crew sprite at native world scale. A visually tidy source plate can still produce a headset larger than a character’s head. Follow shared consumers through both wall directions and inherited rooms; Radio and Listening Post share equipment.
- Match material finish without erasing department colors. Correct broad glossy highlights in source art; global darkening or desaturation does not make machinery matte. Tidal is a finish reference, not a universal geometry or palette template.
- Keep structural hull UVs bound to original textures when repainting an equipment atlas. Register new source contours against existing source regions and retain world layout rectangles. Record small generated canvas differences rather than claiming pixel-perfect geometry.
- Preserve originals, exact prompts, superseded candidates and each selected source hash. Regenerate final registration evidence after a follow-up such as the headphone revision; initial registration reports remain historical.

## Integration and verification

Remove runtime common props from the actual prop list, not just draw calls, so they do not remain obstacles. Respect the editor preview path and keep authoring data reversible. Include copies, selected variants and portable instances in classification. Audit named special renderers and automatic bridge covers as well as shared floor routes.

Classify explicit user-null placements and intentionally suppressed baked dressing before auditing live hosts. Keep a missing-host negative control: filtering deliberate removals must not silence real broken references. Compare specialist rectangles independently from intentional common-prop differences when comparing runtime with Studio.

Refresh cards after the final visual change. Resolve current primary/grid/variant bindings before replacing them; another session may already have installed newer cards. Capture every requested room/rotation, inspect representative native views and preserve the full report. Record exactly which views were visually inspected; captures and hashes do not equal visual acceptance.

Add source/provenance files as well as runtime files; retain rejected candidates without selecting them for runtime. A source manifest or two successful historical test runs do not establish current packaged acceptance.

Sync only the lesson additions to the installed room skill, preserving unrelated concurrent guidance. Record staged versus committed versus pushed separately. Leave a concise handoff with accepted decisions, current selections, exact checks and remaining release/owner-review limits. Do not generate more art during closeout.
